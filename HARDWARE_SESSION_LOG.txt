5. CRITICAL BUGS DIAGNOSED & FIXED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Thread Race Condition in CSV Recorder:
   - Simultaneous writes from RX1 and RX2 reader threads caused row corruption.
   - Fixed by adding a global threading.Lock() around writer.writerow() in 
     scripts/record_multilink_dataset.py.

2. CSV Column Offset Bug in Replay Loader:
   - WiMotion 2.0 CSV files include a 'room_id' column at index 10.
   - Replay loader was slicing row[10:74], attempting to parse 'ROOM_LAB_A' as float.
   - Fixed in spatial/replay_loader.py by slicing row[11:75] for 64 subcarriers.

3. Corrupted Timestamps & Runaway 3.5-Billion Window Loop:
   - 4 malformed rows contained non-epoch timestamps, resulting in total_duration 
     being calculated as 1.79 billion seconds (~3.5 billion sliding windows).
   - Fixed by filtering timestamps to valid epoch range (>1e9) in replay_loader.py, 
     bringing load time down from infinite loop to 6.59 seconds.

4. Feature Extraction Performance Optimization:
   - Added eval_v1=False flag to load_recording_frames to bypass expensive 
     217-feature FFT/SVD operations when training spatial models, enabling 
     instant feature extraction.
